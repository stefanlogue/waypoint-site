Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Waypoint ("the app") is developed by Stefan Logue. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

What the app collects

Location. Waypoint requests access to your location while the app is in use. This is used only to:

  • Show more relevant place search results near you when adding a stop to a route
  • Calculate an accurate distance and duration estimate for a route, starting from your current location

Your location is never stored, logged, or transmitted to any server operated by the developer. It is used in the moment, on your device, to make requests to Apple's MapKit services on your behalf.

What the app stores

Routes and stops you create are stored only on your device. Waypoint does not require an account, does not have a server, and does not sync your data anywhere.

Third-party services

Waypoint uses Apple's MapKit and Maps services to search for places, calculate driving directions, and open routes in the Apple Maps app. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

Children's privacy

Waypoint is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
