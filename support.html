Frequently asked questions

Why does Waypoint need my location?
Your location is used to show more relevant search results when adding a stop, and to calculate an accurate distance/time estimate starting from where you are. It's never stored or sent anywhere beyond that.
Why can I only choose driving directions?
Apple Maps only supports multi-stop routing for driving directions — walking and transit directions in Apple Maps are limited to a single destination. Since Waypoint is built around multi-stop routes, driving is the only mode that works correctly end-to-end.
Does Waypoint work without an internet connection?
Searching for places and calculating driving directions both require an internet connection, since they use Apple's map services. Your saved routes are stored on-device and remain accessible offline.
How do I delete a route?
Swipe left on a route in the list, or open it and use the Edit screen.
Is my data backed up anywhere?
Waypoint doesn't have its own server or account system, so routes are only stored locally on your device.
